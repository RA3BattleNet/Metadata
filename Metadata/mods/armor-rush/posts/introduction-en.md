# Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3 – Armor Rush
## A large-scale mod with a playstyle of its own

Started by ZY in 2014 and updated continuously through version 3.0, Armor Rush is a large-scale mod. It substantially reworks Red Alert 3's combat logic, making battles fiercer and more satisfying.

---

## Core Features

- **Reworked unit combat**: Range baselines are raised sharply, and counter relationships between unit roles are redesigned. The heart of the mod is the clash of powder, steel, and flesh across a vastly expanded battlefield.
- **Expanded tech tiers**: Faction buildings gain tech upgrades that cost credits to purchase. Some of the stronger upgrades can even let Conscripts kill an Apocalypse tank!
- **Optional factions**: Players can choose the three vanilla Red Alert 3 factions, plus six closely related sub-factions. Each has its own exclusive units and upgrade tech. Pick the war machine you like best and join the fight!
- **Match-turning superweapons**: Factions cannot build the vanilla offensive superweapons. In their place, most factions have an ultimate weapon. It takes 10 minutes to prepare, but once fired it can overturn the match. If you want to wipe the enemy off the Earth, these unlimited ultimate weapons will do the job.
- **Relentless Elite AI**: With certain limits lifted, the mod's normal AI is more challenging in every sense. If that still is not enough, take on the Elite AI available to each faction. Elite AI has unique cheat mechanics and exclusive units, and can be deployed on most skirmish maps, turning an ordinary map into a challenge mode on the spot.
- **Rebuilt campaigns**: On top of the vanilla three campaigns of three missions each, the mod brings in Armor Rush units and buildings, and rewrites the story of the existing campaign missions. Enough of a jolt for players who have worn out the vanilla campaigns.
