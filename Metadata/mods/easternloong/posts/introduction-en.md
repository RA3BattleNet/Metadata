# Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3 – EasternLoong
## The Eastern Dragon Rises

*Red Alert 3: EasternLoong* is a massive expansion mod crafted by the Chinese development team "MuStar" based on *Red Alert 3* version 1.12, released in February 2013. Expanding beyond the original three factions, the mod fully realizes a completely independent fourth faction—**"The Heavenly Empire" (Tianchao)**. Featuring an entirely original lore, custom building models, units, and tech tree, it offers players an unprecedented taste of Eastern strategic warfare.

---

## Background Lore

Set before the outbreak of the Third World War, the sleeping Eastern dragon is invaded and occupied by the surging Empire of the Rising Sun. The populace struggles under the dual oppression of a puppet regime and colonial occupiers.

Rising in defiance, various regional factions unite to form the People's Party of the Celestial Empire and establish the Empire Revolutionary Alliance (CRA). Secretly backed by the Soviet Union and the Allied Nations, they wage an arduous war of liberation. After two years of bloody conflict, they drive out the invaders, dismantle the puppet government, and liberate the nation.

---

## Faction Features

* **Eastern Aesthetic Design**: Structures draw inspiration from the grand, fortified motifs of the Great Wall, highlighted by crimson palettes and dragon insignia. Units blend Eastern mecha styling with sci-fi aesthetics for instant visual recognition.
* **Full Chinese Voice Acting**: Every unit features professional Chinese voiceover work, complemented by dedicated unit profile videos matching official production quality.
* **Antimatter Tech Tree**: Centered on antimatter energy, the faction features signature abilities and superweapons such as the Reverse Scale Cannon, Antimatter Shield, and Black Hole Rift—standing toe-to-toe with Allied laser technology, Soviet magnetic tech, and the Empire's psionic weaponry.
* **Airdrop Construction System**: Heavenly Empire structures deploy via orbital airdrops rather than gradual on-site construction, deploying rapidly upon landing and dramatically speeding up base expansion.

---

## Extensive Unit Roster

The mod introduces dozens of brand-new land, sea, and air units, forming a comprehensive tactical ecosystem covering anti-air, anti-armor, siege, and support roles:

* **Infantry & Armor**: Scale Guard, Dark Iron Infantry, Warhawk, Reverse Scale Tank, and the Ba-Hu Heavy Tank.
* **Naval & Aerial Fleet**: Azure Dragon Support Battleship, Sky Fiend Airborne Aircraft Carrier, and Tianzun Submarine.
* **Hero Unit**: **"Lieyan" (Blazing Flame)**, equipped with a unique morale-boosting skill that restores health to allied forces while granting increased attack and movement speed.

---

## Gameplay Highlights

* **Dedicated Top-Secret Protocols**: Spans enhanced training regimens, behind-the-lines logistics, and the devastating ultimate weapon, "Glory of the Republic," covering offense, defense, utility, and annihilation.
* **Dual-Branch Versioning**: Separated into a fine-tuned **Competitive Edition** focused on competitive balance and an **Entertainment Edition** geared toward casual play.
* **Extensive Iteration & Polish**: Honed through numerous patches to balance faction counters, stats, and abilities against the vanilla factions, ensuring a polished experience across solo skirmishes, co-op, and competitive multiplayer.

---

If you want to experience the clash of Eastern advanced technology against Western military might and witness the pinnacle of fan-crafted RTS development, *Red Alert 3: EasternLoong* is an absolute must-play!
