# Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3 – Corona

## The Fourth Faction from Another Timeline

*Red Alert 3: Corona* is a large-scale expansion mod developed by the "Corona MOD Team", a group of Red Alert 3 enthusiasts led by Yifeng, for EA's 2008 real-time strategy game *Command & Conquer: Red Alert 3*. The project started in 2017, opened for public beta in January 2022, and has been continuously updated ever since. It adds an all-new faction, the **Celestial Empire (Shenzhou)**, with a strikingly original art style and gameplay, and delivers a major overhaul and systemic upgrade of the three vanilla factions along with substantial visual improvements. Massive, fully armed capital ships turn naval battles from gunboat skirmishes into true fleet engagements; bombers with tail guns dueling interceptors create an entirely new air combat experience. Beyond balanced standard matches, the "Faction Expansion" mode unlocks a wealth of powerful technologies and units for every faction. Download it and enjoy the gorgeous visuals and the immensely satisfying combinations!

## A Rewritten 1259

After the Third World War, the Empire of the Rising Sun may have been defeated, but it escaped most of the sanctions by playing the Allies and the Soviets against each other. Unwilling to accept a subordinate place, Emperor Tatsu stole the time machine blueprints from Moscow, built the "Gate of Time", and chose 1259 AD as his point of interference. Outside Diaoyu Fortress, he saved Möngke Khan—who was supposed to die there—with a nanite shield, and supplied the Mongol army with technology far beyond its era. He then eliminated the Mongol ruling class at the victory banquet, and the history of Eurasia branched from that moment on. Centuries later, Shenzhou—grown along this independent technological path—enters the world stage as the fourth faction.

## Shenzhou: Controlled Fusion and Quantum Technology

In this world, Shenzhou and the nations of Asia have long led the West in technology and were less influenced by modern Western thought, setting them on a completely different military-technological path.

* **Faction Technology**: Its strengths lie in controlled nuclear fusion and quantum technology. Electromagnetic technology is mostly used to accelerate projectiles or deflect incoming shells rather than to discharge electricity directly like Soviet tesla tech; quantum communications are used for encrypted transmission and even for assembling structures.
* **Standalone System**: It has its own structures, units, commander protocols and construction mechanics. Unit names are largely drawn from Chinese mythology and astronomy, such as the T1 Dragonflame Infantry, the T3 Zhurong Assault Tank, the Jinwu Bomber, the Pojun Golden Armor, the Yaoguang Skyward Cannon, and the naval Qinglong Battle Core Ship.
* **Tech Research**: Once the T3 tech structure "Jiesuan Tower" is complete, you can invest time and funds to research technologies that add capabilities or buffs to specific units; the Qinglong Battle Core Ship's weapon systems are also unlocked through research.
* **Empire of the Rising Sun**: In the Corona universe it remains a major power in Asia, having long learned from Shenzhou in history, and it retains its advanced nanotechnology.

## Gameplay and Modes

* **Unit Stats Retuned**: Movement, range, rate of fire and other values have been readjusted based on playtest results for both balance and realism.
* **New Units and Art Rework**: All three vanilla factions receive new units, including several returning favorites; some vanilla units have been remodeled to unify the overall art style.
* **Tier-4 Tech and Epic Units**: The skirmish tech tree extends to T4, where expensive but powerful T4 units become available.
* **Naval Overhaul**: Capital ships are larger, costlier and more heavily armed, making naval battles bigger and more spectacular.
* **New Combat Systems**: Air combat has been redesigned, with anti-missile and anti-submarine mechanics adding depth to offense and defense.
* **Match Options**: Skirmish lobbies support AI economy multipliers (default, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x), disabled superweapons, economic cheating and more.
* **Faction Expansion**: A non-competitive mode in which all four factions gain extra units, tech and protocols—great for casual games.
